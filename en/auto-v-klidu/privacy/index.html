Auto v klidu works without a user account or operator server. Information you enter is stored locally on your device.

SupportPrivacy

Operator and contact

The app is operated by Adivio s.r.o. For privacy questions, contact info@adivio.cz.

Information the app uses

The app stores vehicle details such as its name, make, model, illustrative color, optional license plate number and VIN, odometer reading and notes. It also stores maintenance tasks, service history, prices, service provider names and attached documents. This information is used to organize and plan vehicle care. Illustrations are included with the installation and are not downloaded while you use the app.

Documents and scanning

You choose documents through the system Files picker. The app accesses only the selected file and saves a local copy in its private storage. If your version offers scanning, you can scan a document with the camera and save it as a local PDF. The camera is used only when you start the scanner. The app does not request access to your entire Photos library. You can choose to share an individual document through the system share sheet with a service you select.

Reminders

Optional reminders use local iOS notifications. Notifications contain a generic maintenance message without the VIN, license plate number or other vehicle details. You can manage notification permission in iOS Settings.

Exports and backups

You create a CSV export of service records only when you choose to do so. It contains task details, odometer readings and entered prices, and may include notes and service provider names. If your version offers a PDF report, you choose the period and can opt to include vehicle details, including the license plate number and VIN, prices, service provider names and notes. Attachments are not included in the PDF report. You choose where to save exported files in the system Files dialog; this may be a cloud service depending on your settings. Saved exports are then subject to the selected provider's terms. CSV and PDF reports are not restorable backups of the entire app.

Depending on iOS settings, local information may be included in a device system backup. Availability and handling of these backups depend on your device settings and the relevant service.

Sharing and data collection

The app has no proprietary backend, analytics, advertising or tracking. The operator does not receive vehicle or maintenance information through the app or provide it to third parties. You control any transfer of an export to a service you select.

Deleting information

You can delete individual records in the app. In Settings → Privacy and Data, you can remove all local information after confirmation, including the vehicle, tasks, history, local document copies and reminders created by the app. This does not delete previous exports, original files outside the app or external backups. Uninstalling the app without an available backup may result in data loss.

Retention and your choices

Local information remains in the app until you delete it or uninstall the app. You can change notification permission, and camera permission in versions with scanning, at any time in iOS Settings. You manage exports and system backups wherever you saved them.

Contacting support

If you email info@adivio.cz, we use your message and contact details to handle your request. We retain the message for as long as needed to respond and meet any legal obligations. You can request access to or deletion of the message at the same address; deletion may be limited by a legal obligation. Do not send VINs, license plate numbers, personal documents or other information that is not needed to resolve the issue.

Changes to this policy

If the way information is handled changes, we will update this policy before the relevant app version is released.
