Frequently asked questions

How do I edit my vehicle details?

Open the vehicle in the overview, choose Edit Details and confirm with Save. You can also change the color of illustrative vehicle presets.

How do maintenance presets work?

A preset fills in a name and category. You set the date, odometer reading and any repetition to suit your vehicle. The task is created only when you save it.

Why didn't I receive a reminder?

Check that the task has a future date and notifications are enabled in iOS Settings. Focus mode and other system settings can affect delivery. An odometer reading alone does not trigger a notification.

How do I add a document?

In Documents or from a vehicle or maintenance item, choose to add a document and select a PDF or image in the system Files app. If your version has a scanner and your device supports it, you can also scan a document as a PDF.

Is an export a backup of the app?

No. CSV is a tabular overview of service records for a selected period. If a PDF report is available in your version, you can choose its period and included information. Reports do not contain attachments or all settings and cannot be imported back into the app. Local information may be included in a device system backup depending on iOS settings.

How do I delete my information?

Open Settings → Privacy and Data in the app. After confirmation, local records, documents and reminders created by the app are deleted. You must delete previously saved exports and external backups separately.
