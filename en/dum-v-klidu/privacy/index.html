SupportPrivacy

Operator and contact

The app is operated by Adivio s.r.o. For privacy questions, contact info@adivio.cz.

Information the app uses

You can enter information about properties, equipment, maintenance, warranties, service costs and contacts, completed work history and attached documents. This information is used only for app features and remains stored locally on your device.

Documents

You choose documents yourself through the iOS file picker. The app accesses only the selected file and saves a local copy in its protected storage. You can also scan documents with the camera. The app requests camera permission only when you start scanning. The system scanner lets you review and crop pages; the result is saved as a local PDF. The app does not automatically recognize information in documents and does not request access to your entire Photos library.

Notifications

Optional reminders are scheduled directly on your device. The app requests notification permission only after you enable a reminder. Notification text does not include task names or other information you entered.

Widget

Through a protected App Group, the widget can access property names and types, names and due dates of active tasks, counts and identifiers needed to open a task. It does not read service costs, contacts, notes or documents. This information stays on your device.

Sharing and data collection

The app has no user account, proprietary backend, advertising, analytics SDK or tracking. The operator does not receive information you enter through the app or provide it to third parties. You can choose to share a document or save a backup with a provider available in the system Files app; you control and confirm that action. The app does not synchronize homes between accounts.

Backups and storage

In Settings → Backup and Restore, you can export properties, history and attachments into one file if it fits the app's size limits. Attachments can total at most 160 MB and the whole file at most 240 MB; the app refuses an export that exceeds either limit. The file is not password protected and may contain contacts and documents. You choose where to save it in Files. You manage and delete copies outside the app with the provider you chose. A backup is restored into an empty database only after validation and confirmation.

Local information may be included in a device backup depending on your Apple service settings. Processing of system backups is governed by Apple's terms and settings. Uninstalling the app without an available backup may result in data loss.

Deleting information

You can remove individual items in the app. You can delete all local information, documents and reminders after confirmation in Settings → Privacy and Data. Because the operator does not receive this information, it has no remote copy to delete or restore on request.

Retention and your choices

Local information remains in the app until you delete it or uninstall the app. You can change camera and notification permissions at any time in iOS Settings. You manage previous exports and system backups wherever they were saved.

Contacting support

If you email info@adivio.cz, we use your message and contact details to handle your request. We retain the message for as long as needed to respond and meet any legal obligations. You can request access to or deletion of the message at the same address; deletion may be limited by a legal obligation. Do not send information or documents that are not needed to resolve the issue.

Changes to this policy

If a future app version changes how information is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released.
