Frequently asked questions

Where is my information?

It is stored locally in the app on your device. Dům v klidu has no account or server synchronization.

How do I add a document?

In the Documents tab or an equipment detail, choose to add a document. Select a PDF or image in Files. On a supported iPhone, you can also use the scanner icon to scan several pages, review their crop and save them as a PDF.

Why didn't a reminder appear at the expected time?

iOS controls delivery of local notifications, which can depend on device settings. Check the app's permission in Settings → Notifications.

How do I delete all information?

Open Settings → Privacy and Data → Delete All Local Data in the app. After confirmation, this removes local records, documents and app reminders.

Can I restore my information?

The app has no proprietary cloud backup. In Settings → Backup and Restore, you can create a portable file containing records and documents, provided attachments total no more than 160 MB and the entire file no more than 240 MB. Restore is available in an empty installation, including during initial setup. It does not overwrite existing records. Keep your own backups; the operator has no remote copy of your information.

How do I add another property or a widget?

At the top of the overview, open the property switcher → Manage Properties → Add Property. Add the widget from the iOS widget gallery and choose a property when editing it. If you archive a property, select another available one in the widget.

Where can I find service costs and warranties?

When completing maintenance, you can enter a price, currency, service company and phone number, and link a document. Maintenance → Service Costs shows totals and a monthly overview by currency; equipment history is also available. In Purchase and Warranty, you can set your own warranty end date and an optional reminder.
