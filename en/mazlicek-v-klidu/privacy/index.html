Mazlíček v klidu
SupportPrivacy

Operator and contact

The app is operated by Adivio s.r.o. For privacy questions, contact info@adivio.cz.

Information the app uses

You enter pet profiles, photos, veterinarian details, care plans, history, expenses, documents and, with Premium, weight and resting breathing records for dogs and cats. Breathing records locally store the date, respiratory rate, measurement method, resting state, optional note and a threshold entered according to your veterinarian’s advice; timer records and older camera records also store the breath count and measurement duration. This information is used for app features and remains stored locally on your device. The app does not determine diagnoses, dosages or vaccination schedules; your veterinarian determines them.

Photos and documents

You select a pet photo using the system Photos picker. The app receives only the selected image and stores a local copy; it does not request access to your entire library. You select documents through the system Files picker, and copies are saved in the app's private storage. The app does not include a document scanner.

Breathing measurements and the earlier test camera

The first public release offers manual counting and entry of existing measurements. It does not access the camera or microphone. Numerical records are stored locally; this is not a continuous monitor or an alarm, and measurement does not continue in the background.

The earlier TestFlight version 1.0 (2) included an experimental camera estimate without demonstrated clinical accuracy. Only in that test build, the rear camera starts after you choose Prepare Camera and grant iOS permission. Images are processed only in device memory, are not saved as photos or video, and are never transmitted. The microphone is not used and audio is not recorded. Saving requires verification by your own counting; only the checked numerical record is stored, not images. You can change permission in iOS Settings. Older numerical records remain readable, deletable and exportable after updating, with their original method label.

Reminders

Notifications are optional and scheduled on your device. The app requests permission after you enable reminders. Notification text may include your pet's name, the care task and its due date; it may appear on the lock screen depending on your iOS settings. You can change permissions and notification previews in iOS Settings.

Subscriptions

Optional Premium is purchased through the App Store. Apple processes payment and provides the information the app needs to verify the purchase and subscription status. The operator does not receive your payment details. Apple's terms govern purchases and subscriptions. Restoring purchases does not restore your local pet records.

Export and sharing

With Premium, you can create a pet card PDF and export history and expenses to CSV. The PDF may contain a photo, microchip number, notes, veterinarian contact, care dates and recent records. For documents, it lists their names and validity dates, not attached file contents. Breathing history can be exported to CSV, including the date, pet name, result, breath count and duration, method, resting state, note and threshold at the time of the record. Breathing history, deletion and CSV export remain available after Premium expires. Exports are created on your device and shared or saved only when you initiate that action through the system interface. You manage copies outside the app with the recipient or provider you choose.

Data collection and transmission

The app has no user accounts, proprietary backend, advertising, analytics SDK or tracking. The operator does not receive information you enter through the app or provide it to third parties. The app does not synchronize records between devices or offer shared family records. Subscription Family Sharing, if enabled in the App Store, does not share pet information.

Storage, backups and deletion

You keep information in the app until you delete it. You can delete individual breathing measurements in their history; deleting a pet profile also removes its measurements and threshold settings. You can remove other individual items or delete all local information, documents and scheduled reminders after confirmation in Settings → Delete all local data. Local information may be included in a system device backup depending on your Apple settings. This version has no built-in complete backup and restore of records. Uninstalling the app without an available system backup may result in data loss. The operator has no remote copy to restore. Deleting all data also includes working copies of shared documents and exports inside the app. If file removal fails, the app shows an error and asks you to repeat deletion. You manage previously shared exports and system backups separately.

Contacting support

If you email info@adivio.cz, we use your message and contact details to handle your request. We retain the message for as long as needed to respond and meet any legal obligations. You can request access to or deletion of the message at the same address; deletion may be limited by a legal obligation. Do not send information or documents that are not needed to resolve the issue.

Changes to this policy

If a future app version changes how information is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released.
