How can we help?

Create a profile

Add a pet, choose its species and enter a name. A photo, breed, date of birth, microchip number and veterinarian contact are optional. The free version supports one pet; Premium supports more profiles. Switch profiles by tapping the avatar on the overview.

Care plans and reminders

In Care, add a custom task or adapt a preset to your pet's species. Confirm dates, repetition and care details with your veterinarian. Enable reminders for individual tasks; if they do not appear, check permissions in iOS Settings. Free reminders arrive at 9 AM. Premium offers a custom time and a second reminder on the due date.

History and expenses

Record completed care or a veterinarian visit in history. Cost is optional; an unspecified cost is not treated as zero. You can attach documents to a record. Premium lets you export history and expenses to CSV.

Photos and documents

Choose a photo from Photos. Add a PDF or document image through Files; the app stores a local copy. You can open and choose to share attachments in Documents. This version does not include a document scanner.

Weight and pet card PDF

With Premium, record weight and view changes in a chart. In the profile, create a pet card PDF for a veterinarian, pet sitter or trip. The card contains information from your records and is not a veterinary document. Check the recipient before sharing.

Premium and restoring purchases

Open Premium in Settings. Monthly and yearly subscriptions unlock the same features; the App Store shows the current price and any introductory offer before you confirm a purchase. If you already subscribe, use Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account. Restoring a purchase does not restore profiles or documents.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Manage your subscription in iOS Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions, or use Manage Subscription in the app. Deleting the app or local information does not cancel your subscription. Terms of use are Apple's standard EULA.

Backups and deletion

Records are local and may be included in an iPhone system backup according to your settings. A built-in complete backup and synchronization between devices are not part of this version. In Settings, you can delete all local information after confirmation; it cannot be restored without a backup.
