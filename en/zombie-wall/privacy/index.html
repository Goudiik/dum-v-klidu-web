Zombie Wall · Privacy policy

Your game, your privacy.

How Zombie Wall stores data, uses Apple services and lets you delete your data.

Zombie Wall
SupportPrivacy

Operator and scope

This policy covers Zombie Wall for iOS and iPadOS, version 1.0.0, and related support. The operator is Adivio s.r.o. For privacy questions or requests about your data, contact info@adivio.cz.

No advertising or tracking in the game

Zombie Wall has no ads, tracking or third-party analytics services. It has no developer-operated user accounts or player database and does not send gameplay data to developer servers. The game does not require access to contacts, location, the camera or the microphone.

Data stored on your device

Saved campaigns, settings, night records and custom character names are stored on your device. They let you continue playing, personalize the game and review past nights. They remain until the relevant data or the app is deleted; some data may also be included in system backups depending on your settings.

iCloud, Game Center and purchases

If you use iCloud, saved campaigns, including in-game names, synchronize between your devices through Apple services. Game Center receives scores and achievements; its settings control sign-in and visibility. The App Store handles the Full Game purchase, restoration and Family Sharing. The game verifies access to purchased content through StoreKit. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Apple provides these services under its privacy policy and your Apple Account settings. You can change iCloud access and Game Center use in system settings. Playing the game does not require an account with the developer.

Sharing and artificial intelligence

A night record is shared only when you open the system share sheet and choose a recipient. The file may contain gameplay events and custom character names. The selected recipient or service also controls how the shared copy is handled. The game sends no data to AI services and generates nothing using AI during gameplay. Some artwork was created with image generation before the game’s release.

Deleting and managing data

You can delete campaigns from the saved games menu; the deletion synchronizes when iCloud is available. Delete the app from your device to remove all local app data (offloading alone does not do this). Also manage system backups, data held by Apple, and iCloud or Game Center use in your Apple Account settings. Deleting the app does not automatically remove previously exported files or all cloud copies. Restoring a purchase does not restore deleted campaigns.

Messages sent to support

If you contact us, we process your email address, message and any attachments to answer your request. The basis is handling your request and our legitimate interest in providing support; any legal obligations are fulfilled separately. We do not provide messages for advertising. We use email service providers to deliver and store email.

We keep messages only as long as needed to resolve your request and meet any legal obligations. To request access, correction or deletion of data in support correspondence, or where applicable restriction of processing or to object, contact info@adivio.cz. We handle requests as required by applicable law; legal obligations may limit deletion of some information. You have the right to contact the relevant data protection supervisory authority.

These web pages

These pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising tracking, forms or first-party cookies. When you visit, the hosting service processes technical information needed to deliver and secure the website, such as your IP address and the requested page address. This is not gameplay data sent by the app. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy rules.

Changes and contact

If features or data handling change, we will update this policy and its date. Questions and requests: info@adivio.cz. For help with playing and purchases, visit support.
