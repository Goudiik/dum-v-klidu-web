About the game and requirements

Zombie Wall is a strategy game for iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 26.0 or later. Build your city and send expeditions by day; defend the wall against the horde at night. The game supports Czech and English. Gameplay works offline; purchases, restoring purchases, iCloud synchronization and online Game Center features require a connection.

What is free, and what does Full Game unlock?

The first 5 campaign nights and the daily challenge are free. A single Full Game purchase unlocks the rest of the 30-night campaign and Endless mode. It is not a subscription. There are no ads, energy systems or waiting timers. The App Store displays the current price in your local currency before you confirm a purchase.

Buying and restoring your purchase

Open the Full Game offer through Endless mode in the main menu or when continuing after night five. If you have already purchased it, choose Restore Purchases on that screen. Use the same Apple Account used for the purchase and an internet connection. Restoring a purchase restores access to content, not a deleted campaign.

If restoration does not help, check that in-app purchases are allowed and restart your device. See Apple’s in-app purchase support for more steps. Apple handles refund requests through Report a Problem. The licence terms are Apple’s standard EULA.

Family Sharing

The Full Game purchase supports Family Sharing under Apple’s rules and your family settings. Access to the full game is shared; family members’ saved campaigns are not merged. If access is missing, check purchase sharing in your Apple Account settings and use Restore Purchases.

Saving and iCloud

Campaigns are stored on your device and synchronized between devices using the same Apple Account when iCloud is available. Check that you are signed in, iCloud is available for the game, and both devices are connected. Synchronization may take time. Game settings and the current night checkpoint remain local; iCloud is not a complete backup of all game data.

You can delete a campaign from the saved games menu. The deletion also reaches your other devices when iCloud is available. If you are troubleshooting lost progress, do not delete the app before contacting support.

Game Center and settings

Game Center is optional and provides scores, leaderboards and achievements. Manage sign-in and visibility in Apple’s Game Center settings. In the game settings you can adjust sound, music, haptics, blood, flashes and camera shake.

Reporting a problem and contact

Operator: Adivio s.r.o. Contact: info@adivio.cz. Include the game version, device model, iOS or iPadOS version and a short description of the steps that cause the problem. Include the game mode and night number if relevant.

Do not send passwords, verification codes or payment details. Check any screenshot or night record before sending it; it may contain custom character names. Our privacy policy explains how we handle messages to support.
